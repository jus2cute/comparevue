## Who Should Use Freshsales?

Small to mid-size sales teams that want a CRM that handles communication natively. Freshsales includes a built-in cloud phone (make calls directly from the CRM), email sync, and live chat — features that would require separate tools (or expensive add-ons) with most CRMs.

The Freddy AI assistant analyzes your deals and gives you lead scores, next-best-action suggestions, and deal insights that help reps prioritize their time. For teams that want to spend less time on CRM admin and more time selling, Freshsales is built for that workflow.

Freshsales is part of the Freshworks ecosystem, so if you also use Freshdesk for support or Freshchat for customer engagement, the native integrations are seamless.

## Who Should Use Pipedrive?

Sales teams that live and breathe pipeline management. Pipedrive's drag-and-drop pipeline is genuinely the best in the industry — it's visual, intuitive, and actually enjoyable to use. The AI Sales Assistant doesn't just show you data; it actively suggests which deals to focus on and what actions to take next.

Pipedrive is purpose-built for sales, not a jack-of-all-trades. If your team's main priority is closing more deals faster (and you already have communication and marketing tools in place), Pipedrive keeps you laser-focused on what matters: moving deals through the pipeline.

The mobile app deserves special mention — it's one of the best CRM mobile experiences, making Pipedrive great for field sales teams who need full pipeline visibility on their phone.
