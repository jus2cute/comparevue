## The Hidden Cost of Salesforce

Salesforce's sticker price is misleading. Between implementation, ongoing admin, training, and add-on products, the total cost of ownership is typically 3-5x the subscription price. HubSpot's all-in-one model is far more predictable, especially for smaller teams.

For a 10-person sales team:
- **HubSpot:** ~$1,000/month (Professional CRM + basic marketing)
- **Salesforce:** ~$800/month subscription, but $2,000-$5,000/month including implementation amortization, admin time, and training

## Who Should Use HubSpot?

HubSpot is built for growing teams that want a unified platform — marketing, sales, service, and CMS in one place, with a single login and consistent UX. The free CRM is genuinely capable, and the interface is intuitive enough that sales reps actually want to use it (a surprisingly rare quality in CRMs).

The key advantage is the all-in-one model. As you grow, you can add Marketing Hub, Service Hub, and CMS without switching platforms or dealing with disjointed integrations.

## Who Should Use Salesforce?

Salesforce is the enterprise standard for a reason. Its customization depth is unmatched — you can model virtually any business process with custom objects, flows, and validation rules. AppExchange gives you a marketplace of 4,000+ extensions for nearly any need.

But — and it's a big but — you need a Salesforce admin or consultant to get the most out of it. The platform is powerful but not self-serve. Best for mid-market and enterprise teams with dedicated operations resources who can invest in proper setup and maintenance.
