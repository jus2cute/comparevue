## Quick Verdict

Choose **Monday.com** if you need high customizability and visual dashboards. Choose **Asana** if you want workflows, timeline views, and smoother team collaboration out of the box. For small teams on a budget, Asana's generous free plan (up to 15 users) is hard to beat.

## Feature Comparison

Monday.com and Asana both cover the project management basics — task management, timelines, team collaboration — but they approach them differently.

| Feature | Monday.com | Asana |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| Visual boards | ✅ 15+ view types | ✅ Boards, lists, timeline, Gantt |
| Automation | ✅ 200+ templates | ✅ Rules-based, intuitive builder |
| Time tracking | ❌ Requires add-on | ✅ Built-in |
| Free plan | Up to 2 seats | Up to 15 users |
| Integrations | 200+ | 300+ |

## Pricing at a Glance

| Plan | Monday.com | Asana |
|------|-----------|-------|
| Free | $0 (2 seats) | $0 (15 users) |
| Starter | $9/seat/mo | $10.99/user/mo |
| Pro | $19/seat/mo | $24.99/user/mo |

## Who Should Use Monday.com?

Teams that value visual customization, colorful dashboards, and flexible board structures. Great for marketing teams, creative agencies, and operations teams that want total control over their workflows.

## Who Should Use Asana?

Teams that need structured workflows, timeline planning, and cross-functional coordination. Strong for product teams, engineering departments, and mid-to-large organizations that rely on dependencies and milestones.

## Bottom Line

For teams of 3-15 people who want to spend $0 and get started immediately, **Asana wins**. If your team is 2-5 people and you're willing to pay for serious customization and visual dashboards, go with **Monday.com**.
