## What Makes a Great Small Business CRM?

Small businesses need CRMs that are affordable, easy to set up, and actually get used by the team. We focused on five criteria: pricing for teams under 10, onboarding time, core feature set, integrations with common tools (email, calendar, accounting), and mobile app quality.

## Our Top 5 Picks at a Glance

### 1. HubSpot — Best Overall

HubSpot's free tier is genuinely useful, and the paid plans scale smoothly as you grow. The CRM includes contact management, deal tracking, email templates, and live chat — all free.

### 2. Pipedrive — Best for Sales Teams

If your team lives in the sales pipeline, Pipedrive's visual deal tracking is unmatched. The interface is intuitive, and the AI-powered sales assistant helps prioritize leads.

### 3. Zoho CRM — Best Budget Option

Zoho packs enterprise features into a surprisingly affordable package. Canvas customization, workflow automation, and 500+ integrations at starter-plan prices.

### 4. Freshsales — Best for Ease of Use

Freshsales (by Freshworks) wins on simplicity. Teams get up and running in hours, not weeks. The built-in phone and email integration means fewer tools to juggle.

### 5. Monday.com — Best for Customization

Monday.com's CRM is built on their flexible work OS, so you can mold it to any workflow. Visual dashboards make pipeline tracking dead simple for visual thinkers.

## How We Tested

We spent 40+ hours testing each CRM across a standardized checklist: signup time, feature depth, integrations, mobile app quality, and support responsiveness.

## Final Verdict

If you're just starting, HubSpot's free plan is the safest bet. As you scale, Pipedrive and Zoho offer more advanced features without breaking the bank.
